About

More about me.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at the University of Florida, working at the intersection of applied mathematics, scientific computing, biological modeling, and optimization under uncertainty.

Background

From mathematical theory to usable tools.

My current research focuses on nonlinear dynamical systems, uncertainty quantification, sensitivity analysis, and optimal control. I am also interested in the mathematical foundations of AI and machine learning.

Alongside my dissertation work, I build scientific software designed to make advanced analysis more accessible, reproducible, and useful to researchers.

Direction

Research with broader quantitative applications.

My broader interests include machine learning, reinforcement learning, quantitative research, model validation, and robust decision-making.

Capabilities

Methods and tools I work with.

Mathematical Modeling

Nonlinear ODE systems, biological mechanisms, calibration, validation, and interpretation.

Optimization

Optimal control, robust optimization, constrained decision problems, and computational methods.

Technical Tools

MATLAB, Python, Git, LaTeX, Streamlit, and data visualization.
