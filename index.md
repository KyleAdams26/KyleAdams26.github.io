# About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at the University of Florida (UF).

My research focuses on mathematical modeling, uncertainty
quantification, and robust optimal control. I have also recently become interested in machine learning.

I plan to be a triple Gator! Before grad school, I double majored in mathematics and statistics at UF, too. I took one gap year to teach high school mathematics in Tampa, FL, and had a great time. I love to mentor and teach, especially anyone who doesn't feel that math has been welcoming to them. I firmly believe anyone can be a mathematician.

In my free time, I love to learn new things, could be math or not math! Currently, I am learning how to garden, and am about to plant broccoli and brussels sprouts for the first time (October 2026).
