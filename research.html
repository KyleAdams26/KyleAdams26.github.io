Research

Understanding complex systems under uncertainty.

My work develops mathematical and computational methods for studying biological systems, identifying influential mechanisms, and designing decisions that remain effective when model parameters are uncertain.

01

Mechanistic Modeling

Developing nonlinear dynamical systems that represent interactions among tumor, immune, and stromal cell populations.

02

Uncertainty Quantification

Using global sensitivity analysis and uncertainty propagation to identify the mechanisms that most strongly determine model outcomes.

03

Robust Optimal Control

Designing treatment strategies that perform reliably across uncertain parameters and heterogeneous patient scenarios.
