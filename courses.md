# Courses

These courses are freely available and designed for students interested in learning mathematics through video lectures and supplementary materials.

## Linear Algebra

This course provides an introduction to linear algebra, emphasizing both computational methods and mathematical reasoning. Topics include systems of linear equations, matrices, vector spaces, linear transformations, determinants, eigenvalues, and diagonalization.

The course is intended for undergraduate students and anyone interested in developing a rigorous foundation in linear algebra.

### Textbook

Friedberg, S. H., Insel, A. J., and Spence, L. E. *Linear Algebra*.
